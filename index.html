The problem

Triage eats your team's time

🔍

Read every ticket manually

Someone opens each ticket, reads the description, checks attachments, and figures out what kind of issue it is.

👥

Hunt for the right person

Then they check who is available, who knows the client, who is already overloaded, and who has the right skills.

⏰

10 to 15 minutes, every time

This process repeats for every single ticket. It is repetitive, slow, and pulls your best people away from real work.

⚠️

Wrong assignments cost more

Assign to someone without client context and they spend the first hour just understanding the environment.

The flow

From ticket to assignment in seconds

01

Read the ticket Jira API

The agent fetches the real ticket from Jira. Summary, description, priority, client name, reporter. One API call, all the context.

02

Classify the issue AI reasoning

Claude reads the unstructured description and classifies it. Technical bug, business request, or general question. This is the one step that genuinely needs LLM judgment.

03

Route to the right team deterministic

Category maps to a team. This is a lookup table, not AI guessing. A deliberate design choice that keeps it fast and reliable.

04

Match availability + client history AI reasoning

Who on the team has bandwidth? Who has worked with this client before? For critical tickets, the agent can pull someone off lower priority work.

05

Send to Slack for review Slack SDK

The recommendation goes to your Slack channel with full reasoning. Who, why, and what the ticket is about. You read it where you already work.

06

You approve in Slack human in the loop

Type "approved" in Slack. The webhook captures your real display name automatically. Nothing happens to Jira until you say so.

07

Audit trail written to Jira Jira API

A structured comment is posted to the ticket: recommended assignee, full reasoning, and your name as the approver. Permanent and searchable.

Why this is different

Most tools classify.
This one assigns.

Existing tools

Stop at classification and priority

  • Classify ticket type and assign priority
  • Suggest a team or routing queue
  • Generate a response template
  • No client relationship awareness
  • No capacity checking per person
  • No approval gate or audit trail

This agent

Recommends a specific person, safely

  • Matches to a person, not just a team
  • Weighs client history as a routing factor
  • Checks real availability before recommending
  • Applies severity logic for edge cases
  • Gates every write behind your approval
  • Captures your name in the Jira audit trail

Evolution

Built in iterations, not all at once

v1

MCP agent with Jira and Slack integration

Agent reads real Jira tickets, applies triage logic, sends Slack notification. Approval happens in Claude Desktop, then comment is written to Jira.

v2

Slack webhook approval with named audit trail

Human approves directly in Slack. Webhook server captures the approver's real display name and writes it to the Jira comment. No Claude Desktop needed for approval.

v3

Fully automated end to end

Jira webhook triggers triage automatically on ticket creation. Team availability pulled from live data. Zero manual steps from ticket creation to assignment recommendation.
